Why publishers use it

Canva and Word don't know KDP's gutter rules — most rejected interiors fail on margins. BlankPress sets the inside margin from your page count (0.375"–0.875") and the outside margin for bleed/no-bleed automatically, mirrors it for left and right pages, and keeps the file tiny by sharing identical pages.

FAQ

Is the PDF accepted by KDP?

Yes. Page size matches the trim (plus 0.125" bleed when enabled), margins follow KDP's minimums by page count, and fonts are the standard PDF base-14 set. Always run KDP's previewer before publishing — it is the final judge.

Do my files get uploaded?

No. The PDF is generated entirely in your browser and saved to your computer. We never see it.

Are the sudoku puzzles unique?

Every sudoku is generated with exactly one solution and verified before it is placed. Word searches and mazes are seeded, so the same settings reproduce the same book.

Can I sell the books I make?

Yes. Everything you generate is yours to publish commercially, free or Pro.
