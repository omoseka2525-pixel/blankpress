Privacy Policy

BlankPress runs entirely in your browser. The settings you choose and the PDFs you generate never leave your device and are never sent to our servers.

What we collect

Nothing on the site itself. If you buy Pro, payment is processed by Stripe, Inc.; Stripe shares your email and receipt with us so we can support you. We do not see card numbers.

Local storage

Your Pro status is stored in your browser's local storage so you stay unlocked. Clearing site data removes it; enter your code again to restore.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Payments: Stripe. We do not use advertising trackers.
